## How AI for Faculty Got Started

AI for Faculty grows from the [GOAL Framework](https://www.goalframework.org/ai-academic-integrity-and-the-goal-framework/)'s
commitment to growth, authenticity, and equity. It's the philosophical
ground from which all our work — teaching, writing, organizing — continues
to grow.
