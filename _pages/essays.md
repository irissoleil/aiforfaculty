Essays are where I step back from the relentless swirl of headlines and case studies to think aloud. Instead of tracing a single development or policy, I'll linger on the tensions, questions, and convictions that inform and inspire my work with AI and higher ed. They’re more reflective and more personal, drawing on field reports, campus experience, and conversation to explore how this moment is reshaping what we value, how we teach, and what we owe our students and each other.
